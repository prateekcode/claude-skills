Activation · Q2

The checklist works, but almost nobody finishes it

People who complete the onboarding checklist convert at 31.8% against 12.5% for those who never open it — but only 4.2% finish it, roughly 1 in 24. The lever is real and it is barely being pulled.

Everything below covers the 17,397 accounts created between April and June that reached the dashboard at least once. Accounts that bounced before first render are excluded.

Where people stop

Each step is drawn against the full cohort, so the bars shrink as people drop out. The gap between step two and step three is where the funnel actually breaks.

Opened checklist 48.1%
Connected a source 22.6%
Invited a teammate 6.9%
Finished all four 4.2%

Bars are true to scale against the whole cohort. The last bar is meant to look nearly empty — that is the finding, not a rendering problem.

Does finishing actually cause the conversion?

Partly. A holdout that never saw the checklist converts at 12.5%, so the finishers' 31.8% is not all selection effect — but finishers were already the more committed accounts. Treat the honest lift as the middle of that range, not the full 19.3-point gap.

Finished checklist 31.8%
Opened, didn't finish 18.4%
Never saw it (holdout) 12.5%

What to do

Step three — inviting a teammate — costs more than two thirds of everyone still standing at step two. It is also the only step that requires knowing someone else's email address on the spot. Make it skippable and re-offer it a week later, then re-run this read-out against the same cohort definition.
